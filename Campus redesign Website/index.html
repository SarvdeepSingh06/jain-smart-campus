CAMPUS GUIDE & DIRECTORY

Platform Information & Assistance

Learn how JAIN Smart Campus handles facility requests, manages lost belongings, and protects student privacy.

What is JAIN Smart Campus?

Overview & Purpose

JAIN Smart Campus is the university's centralized digital operations platform. It replaces scattered informal WhatsApp groups, misplaced paper slips, and delayed office inquiries with a transparent, mobile-first web utility accessible across the entire institution.

The platform coordinates facility repairs and manages lost belongings through direct communication with on-duty university personnel.

What Lost@JAIN Does

Belongings Protocol

Lost@JAIN operates a dual workflow designed to minimize student stress and prevent theft:

  • Reporting Lost Items: Students describe misplaced belongings, location, and contact details. Reports are immediately logged for security matching.
  • Reporting Found Items: Anyone discovering unattended property logs it and deposits it at the nearest verified campus station (e.g. Main Gate 1 Security or Library Desk).

What Fix-it QR Does

Campus Ticketing

Fix-it QR turns physical campus spaces into interactive repair points. Scannable QR stickers located on classroom podiums, lab benches, cafeteria walls, and hostel notice boards automatically populate the exact room, floor, and building.

Reports are categorized with urgency levels so safety hazards (e.g. electrical shorts, water leaks) receive immediate priority.

How to Submit Reports

Step-by-Step Guide
  1. Scan or Select: Scan the nearest room QR sticker with your phone camera, or choose either Lost@JAIN or Fix-it QR from the homepage.
  2. Provide Details: Complete the clean form with the specific room, issue type, and an optional reference photo.
  3. Receive Reference ID: Get an instant unique tracking ID (e.g. FIX-8A2F1 or LOST-LF102) to follow up on the Insights page.

What Happens After Submission?

Operational Routing

Your ticket is entered into the university's digital queue:

  • Maintenance: Routed to on-duty electricians, carpenters, or AV technicians on their current floor rotation.
  • Lost & Found: Logged into the central security registry. Turned-in items are inspected and categorized daily.

How Reports Are Resolved

Verification & Closeout

When a technician repairs an issue, they verify operation and mark the ticket Fixed. For lost items, once ownership is verified and the item is handed over to the rightful student, the listing is closed and archived.

Ownership Verification & Privacy

Data Security

Your Privacy is Guaranteed: Student phone numbers and email addresses submitted on report forms are never displayed publicly on boards. They are accessible only to university staff contacting you regarding your report.

Anti-Fraud Verification: To claim an item from the Security Desk, claimants must state unique identifying details (such as stickers, wallpaper, lock patterns, or internal markings) that are intentionally withheld from public listings.
